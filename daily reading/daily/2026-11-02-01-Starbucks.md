## 长难句拆解

> ⚠️ 下面这句是按本文数据仿写的典型句（非原文），用来练结构：

> **Starbucks said** U.S. comparable sales **rose** 7.7% in the quarter, **topping** the 6.5% estimate, **while** international comparable sales **rose** just 0.9%, **short of** the 3.2% consensus, **as** traffic declines **offset** higher average ticket.

**三步拆：**

1. **找真谓语**：`said`（主句唯一真谓语）
2. **主干**：**Starbucks said (that) …**
3. **挂回去**：
   - `U.S. comparable sales rose 7.7%` → 宾语从句 1
   - `topping the 6.5% estimate` → 现在分词，表结果（= which topped…）
   - `while international comparable sales rose just 0.9%` → 对比状语从句
   - `short of the 3.2% consensus` → 形容词短语，补语
   - `as traffic declines offset higher average ticket` → 原因状语从句（`offset` 抵消）

**读懂顺序**：星巴克说 → 美国同店 +7.7%（超预期）→ 国际只 +0.9%（远不及预期）→ 因为客流下滑抵消了客单价上涨。

## 第三遍 · 摘要范例（英文，2–3 句）

> Starbucks reported mixed fiscal fourth-quarter results: U.S. same-store sales rose 7.7% and beat estimates, but international comparable sales grew just 0.9%, well short of the 3.2% consensus, as weaker traffic offset higher average tickets. Adjusted earnings topped forecasts at 80 cents a share, while revenue of $9.14 billion came in slightly below expectations. For fiscal 2027, the company guided to adjusted EPS above consensus but projected a 1% decline in revenue.
